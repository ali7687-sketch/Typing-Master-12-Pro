How to Increase Your Typing Speed (WPM)

Touch typing is the technique of typing without looking at the keyboard. By keeping your index fingers anchored on the home row keys (F and J), your brain develops muscle memory, allowing you to type faster with fewer errors.

Understanding Words Per Minute (WPM)

Typing speed is measured in Words Per Minute (WPM). Standard WPM calculation assumes an average word length of 5 characters. Net WPM factors in your uncorrected errors to give you an accurate measure of your actual productivity.

Average Typing Speed Benchmarks

  • Beginner: 20 – 30 WPM
  • Average Office Worker: 40 – 50 WPM
  • Professional Typist / Programmer: 65 – 90+ WPM
