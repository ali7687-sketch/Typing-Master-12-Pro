How to Improve Your Typing Speed (WPM)

Touch typing is the ability to type without looking at the keyboard. By resting your fingers on the home row keys (ASDF and JKL;), your brain develops muscle memory over time.

What is a good typing speed?

The average typing speed is around 40 WPM (Words Per Minute). Professional typists usually reach between 65 and 90 WPM.
